Stream Kit

Animated player cards for o-b-j vs Gank Me Harder Daddy Kaleb, on a transparent background, ready for OBS. The cards load live from the site, so they show each player's current card, record and hero.

Fastest: import the scenes

  1. Download OBS scenes
  2. In OBS: Scene Collection → Import, pick the file, then Scene Collection → DSL Champion Final.
  3. You get a scene per team (all five cards in a row) and a scene per player (one big card). Copy any card source into your own scenes.

Or add cards one by one

In OBS add a Browser Source, paste a link, and set width 600, height 840. Right-click the source and choose Refresh to pick up card changes.

Any other player

Add ?stream=card to the end of their profile link on the site, e.g. …/champion/player/124583057?stream=card. Signed in, you'll also see a "Copy stream link" box on their profile.
