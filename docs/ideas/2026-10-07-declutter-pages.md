# Declutter every page (idea, 2026-10-07; not started)

Origin: Fav, from the Discord thread with Kiara: some stats are cool, others are fluff. "It's ok to
have less stuff visible." Plan for the Content highlights, then every page. Make no changes until
the user breaks it down further.

## Rule
The default view of a page answers one question in a few items. Everything else sits one click
away (a "More" tab or a separate fluff page). Nothing is deleted.

## Content highlights
- Keep (story): Player of the week / MVPs, biggest comeback or lead thrown, best laner, biggest
  teamfight swing, and a roam that won or cost its lane (only when it fires).
- Fluff page: top GPM, most kills, biggest damage game, wards, stacks, streaks, other raw totals.
- Rate a stat against the player's usual output so one hero doesn't win it every week.
- Show the top 4-5 cards; the rest behind "more highlights".

## Page by page (first pass from tabs and headings, not a stat audit)
- Game page: 8 tabs, ~10 sections. Up front: Scoreboard, Draft, Laning. Fold map layers and
  Smokes / Vision / Towers detail into one "Deep stats" tab.
- Players: keep Tier list and Laning; Stat leaders, Medal vs rating, All stats under "More".
- Heroes: Hero tiers first; the other two stay secondary.
- Teams / Standings: Table and Matches default; Crosstable and Race secondary.
- Predict: mostly fine (task-driven).

Order: Content highlights, game page, Players, then review Teams / Heroes / Predict.
